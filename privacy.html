Privacy Policy

Two Lamps · Last updated 22 November 2026

Two Lamps has no servers, no accounts and no tracking. What you put into the app stays in your own iCloud, and is shared only with the people in your reading groups.

Who is responsible

Two Lamps is made by Magnus Løseth, a private developer in Norway. If you have questions about your data, write to mloseth@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is stored in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. I cannot see it, and it is never sent to me. Apple stores it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

  • Your books: title, author, edition, ISBN and cover, and when you started and finished them. Only you can see these, unless you choose to show the book you're reading to a group.
  • Your reading time: when you read and for how long. Only you can see this.
  • Your groups: the group's name, reading days, start time, length and time zone.

What the people in your groups can see

When you create or join a group, it is shared through iCloud with the people in that group, and only with them. They can see:

  • the name you use in the group,
  • when your lamp is lit, paused or out, and which days you read this week,
  • the title and author of the book you're reading, if you choose to show it.

You can leave a group at any time. If you made the group, you can remove people or delete it.

Book search

When you search for a book, your search words or ISBN are sent to Open Library, a free book catalogue run by the Internet Archive, and cover images are loaded from it. Open Library sees these requests and your IP address, as any website would, but nothing that identifies you in the app. Its own terms and privacy policy apply.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your phone: a reminder at your group's reading time, and a silent notice when your reading time is up. They are created on the device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off for each group in the app, or for the whole app in Settings.

Purchases

Any purchase in the app is handled by Apple. I receive no payment details.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No account, password or email address.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

If you choose to share crash reports with developers in your iPhone's settings, or you test the app through TestFlight, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage reports with me. I use them only to fix problems.

Your rights, and deleting your data

Because your data is in your own iCloud, you are in control of it. Delete a book or a group in the app, leave a group, or remove the app's data under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Deleting the app without removing its iCloud data keeps your books for the next time you install it.

If you live in the EU or EEA, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion. Since I hold no data about you, the steps above are the way to use them. You can also contact me, or complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
